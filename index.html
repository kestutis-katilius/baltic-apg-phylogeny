Scope, name changes and interpretation

The 104 flowering-plant family names in the source become 103 APG V families: Phrymaceae is included in Orobanchaceae (APG V, section 6, point 24). Both original family names remain linked to the merged tip. No database names were changed. Counts include accepted species in the stored regional snapshot, including introduced and hybrid taxa; they are not restricted to current native, non-doubtful records. One species is inferred from a Baltic subspecies. The geographic unit is TDWG BLT: Estonia, Latvia, Lithuania and Kaliningrad; individual-country presence is not established.

The paper's family sequence is used for tip ordering only; it is not converted into an invented binary family tree. Order topology was manually transcribed from Figure 1, and all 407 family entries in Appendix B were parsed and checked before matching the Baltic subset. Original names and classifications are provided in the companion mapping file.
